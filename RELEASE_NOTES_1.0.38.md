# Safeer Linux 1.0.38

**Programs you start from the TV get their own screen, and your desktop stays yours.**

- **Separate screen for the TV (Safeer Control 2.0.4).** A program you start from Safeer OS now opens on a second, invisible screen on the computer instead of on your desktop. The TV shows and controls only that screen: your monitor, mouse, keyboard and taskbar keep working as usual, and the program's sound plays on the TV only. The picture is encoded by the graphics card; on our test laptop (Intel Iris Xe) it reaches the TV at 1920×1080 and 60 frames per second.
- **One-click install.** The safeer-control package brings what this needs (`sway`, `wf-recorder`, `wtype`) with it, so a double-click on the package (or `sudo apt install ./safeer-control_2.0.4_all.deb`) is all it takes. If one of them is missing later or wf-recorder is too old, Safeer Control offers to install or update it from its tray menu — only when you click it and confirm, and the system asks for your password. Older wf-recorder versions also work now, without the options they do not know.
- **Several programs on the TV screen.** "Next program" in the session menu on the TV switches between them, also out of a full-screen game.
- **Games start with the arrows as arrows.** A game started from the TV opens in key mode instead of pointer mode.
- **Close programs from the TV.** A program started from the TV can be closed from the Continue row in Safeer OS, one by one or all at once.
- **No more dark screen.** When the last program on the TV screen closes (for example a game that quits on Escape), the TV returns to Safeer OS and says so.
- Fixed: ending a session while the gamepad was in use could leave an error behind in Safeer Control.
- Safeer Browser has no functional changes in this release; filtering, Safeer Link and the interface work as in 1.0.36.

Slovensko: **programi, ki jih zaženeš s televizorja, dobijo svoj zaslon.** Safeer Control 2.0.4 jih odpre na drugem, nevidnem zaslonu računalnika namesto na tvojem namizju; televizor vidi in upravlja samo tega, tvoj monitor, miška, tipkovnica in opravilna vrstica pa delajo kot vedno, zvok programa pa se predvaja samo na televizorju. Sliko kodira grafična kartica; na našem preizkusnem prenosniku (Intel Iris Xe) pride na televizor v 1920×1080 pri 60 sličicah na sekundo. **Namestitev z enim klikom:** paket safeer-control s seboj namesti, kar je za to potrebno (`sway`, `wf-recorder`, `wtype`); če kaj od tega kasneje manjka ali je wf-recorder prestar, Safeer Control v meniju v pladnju ponudi namestitev ali posodobitev – samo na tvoj klik in potrditev, geslo pa vpišeš v sistemsko okno. Deluje tudi s starejšimi različicami wf-recorderja. Med več programi preklopiš z »Naslednji program« v meniju seje, igre se odprejo v načinu tipk, programe zapreš iz vrstice Nadaljuj v Safeer OS (posamično ali vse), in ko se zadnji program zapre, se televizor vrne v Safeer OS namesto na temen zaslon. Popravljeno: konec seje med uporabo igralnega ploščka je lahko v Safeer Controlu pustil napako. Brskalnik Safeer v tej izdaji nima funkcionalnih sprememb.

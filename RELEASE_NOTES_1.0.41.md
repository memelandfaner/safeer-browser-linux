# Safeer Linux 1.0.41

**Programs you start from the TV get their own screen, and your desktop stays yours.**

- **Separate screen for the TV (Safeer Control 2.0.7).** A program you start from Safeer OS now opens on a second, invisible screen on the computer instead of on your desktop. The TV shows and controls only that screen: your monitor, mouse, keyboard and taskbar keep working as usual, and the program's sound plays on the TV only. The picture is encoded by the graphics card; on our test laptop (Intel Iris Xe) it reaches the TV at 1920×1080 and 60 frames per second.
- **One-click install.** The safeer-control package brings what this needs (`sway`, `wf-recorder`, `wtype`) with it, so a double-click on the package (or `sudo apt install ./safeer-control_2.0.7_all.deb`) is all it takes. If one of them is missing later or wf-recorder is too old, Safeer Control offers to install or update it from its tray menu — only when you click it and confirm, and the system asks for your password. Older wf-recorder versions also work now, without the options they do not know.
- **Several programs on the TV screen.** "Next program" in the session menu on the TV switches between them, also out of a full-screen game.
- **The controller's D-pad jumps between buttons.** In a program on the TV screen (pointer mode) the D-pad moves to the next button or field in that direction and A clicks it — in the calculator you walk over the digits and operations. In a game (key mode) the D-pad stays the arrow keys, held as long as you hold it, and the sticks always move the mouse.
- **The remote jumps between buttons, like on Android.** In a program on the TV screen a short press of an arrow on the remote jumps to the next button, link or field in that direction; holding the arrow moves the pointer freely, and a short press never throws it past its target any more. Brave, Chrome and other Chromium browsers started from the TV take part too.
- **Ending the session closes the program.** When you end the session on the TV (hold Back, or "End" in the menu), the programs it started are closed on the computer too, instead of running on in the background. A program with unsaved work may still ask, and then stays open in the Continue row.
- **Exact frame and click (Safeer Control 2.0.7).** Selection reads only the window in front and corrects the invisible shadow GTK4 programs draw around their windows — before, the frame and the click landed 20 points too low and to the right. Left and right stay in the same row (down goes to the next one). A dialog starts on its default button, OK keeps the frame on the same button, and at the edge of a page the page scrolls.
- **Programs do not stay behind:** if the TV disappears in the middle of a session (switched off, app closed) and does not come back within 90 seconds, the programs on the separate screen close, as if the session had ended.
- **Cleaner separate screen:** programs started from the TV fill the screen without a tab bar; coloured remote keys work in Brave, Chrome and other Chromium browsers.
- **Tidy exit:** quitting Safeer Control or logging out also closes the separate screen with its programs, instead of leaving them running unseen. A TV that asks for a program that is no longer open is told so, and never gets your desktop instead.
- **Games start with the arrows as arrows.** A game started from the TV opens in key mode instead of pointer mode.
- **Close programs from the TV.** A program started from the TV can be closed from the Continue row in Safeer OS, one by one or all at once.
- **No more dark screen.** When the last program on the TV screen closes (for example a game that quits on Escape), the TV returns to Safeer OS and says so.
- Fixed: ending a session while the gamepad was in use could leave an error behind in Safeer Control.
- Safeer Browser has no functional changes in this release; filtering, Safeer Link and the interface work as in 1.0.36.

Slovensko: **programi, ki jih zaženeš s televizorja, dobijo svoj zaslon.** Safeer Control 2.0.7 jih odpre na drugem, nevidnem zaslonu računalnika namesto na tvojem namizju; televizor vidi in upravlja samo tega, tvoj monitor, miška, tipkovnica in opravilna vrstica pa delajo kot vedno, zvok programa pa se predvaja samo na televizorju. Sliko kodira grafična kartica; na našem preizkusnem prenosniku (Intel Iris Xe) pride na televizor v 1920×1080 pri 60 sličicah na sekundo. **Namestitev z enim klikom:** paket safeer-control s seboj namesti, kar je za to potrebno (`sway`, `wf-recorder`, `wtype`); če kaj od tega kasneje manjka ali je wf-recorder prestar, Safeer Control v meniju v pladnju ponudi namestitev ali posodobitev – samo na tvoj klik in potrditev, geslo pa vpišeš v sistemsko okno. Deluje tudi s starejšimi različicami wf-recorderja. Med več programi preklopiš z »Naslednji program« v meniju seje, igre se odprejo v načinu tipk, programe zapreš iz vrstice Nadaljuj v Safeer OS (posamično ali vse), in ko se zadnji program zapre, se televizor vrne v Safeer OS namesto na temen zaslon. **Križec kontrolerja skače po gumbih:** v programu na zaslonu televizorja (način kazalca) križec skoči na naslednji gumb ali polje v tisti smeri, A ga klikne – v kalkulatorju se sprehajaš po številkah in operacijah; v igri (način tipk) so križec puščice, palici pa vedno premikata miško. **Daljinec skače po gumbih kot na Androidu:** kratek pritisk puščice skoči na naslednji gumb, povezavo ali polje, držanje pa kazalec vodi prosto – kratek pritisk ga ne odnese več mimo cilja; to velja tudi za Brave, Chrome in druge brskalnike Chromium. **Konec seje zapre program:** ko sejo na televizorju končaš (drži Nazaj ali »Končaj« v meniju), se programi, ki jih je zagnala, zaprejo tudi na računalniku; program z neshranjenim delom lahko še vpraša in ostane v vrstici Nadaljuj. **Natančen okvir in klik (Safeer Control 2.0.7):** izbira bere samo okno v ospredju in popravi nevidno senco okoli oken programov GTK4 – prej sta okvir in klik pristala 20 točk prenizko in preveč desno; levo in desno ostaneta v isti vrsti (v naslednjo gre dol), pogovorno okno začne na privzetem gumbu, na robu strani se stran premakne. **Programi ne ostajajo odprti:** če televizor sredi seje izgine (ugasnjen, zaprta aplikacija) in se v 90 sekundah ne vrne, se programi na ločenem zaslonu zaprejo, kot bi se seja končala. Ločen zaslon je brez vrstice zavihkov. **Urejen izhod:** ob izhodu iz Safeer Controla ali odjavi se zapre tudi ločen zaslon s programi; televizor, ki zahteva program, ki ni več odprt, to izve in nikoli ne dobi namesto njega tvojega namizja. Popravljeno: konec seje med uporabo igralnega ploščka je lahko v Safeer Controlu pustil napako. Brskalnik Safeer v tej izdaji nima funkcionalnih sprememb.

# Safeer Linux 1.0.32

**Safeer Link: control your TV from this computer — and let your phone control this computer.**

- Click the TV in the Safeer Link window and choose Remote: keys (Home, Back, play/pause, D-pad), volume, the TV's apps with icons, a screenshot of its screen; type a command, a page address or a search — "open YouTube", "louder", "channel 25", "play Siddharta".
- Your phone or TV can control Safeer on this computer the same way: open pages, scroll, play or pause, computer volume, a screenshot of the open tab.
- Everything travels over your home network through Safeer Link; nothing extra to install, no cloud.

Slovensko: V oknu Safeer Link klikni televizor in izberi Daljinec: tipke (Domov, Nazaj, predvajaj/pavza, smerni križ), glasnost, aplikacije televizorja z ikonami, posnetek njegovega zaslona; vtipkaš ukaz, naslov strani ali iskanje – »odpri YouTube«, »glasneje«, »program 25«, »predvajaj Siddharta«. Enako lahko telefon ali televizor upravlja Safeer na tem računalniku: odpre stran, drsi, predvaja ali ustavi, nastavi glasnost, pokaže posnetek zavihka. Vse gre po domačem omrežju prek Safeer Linka; nič dodatnega za namestitev, brez oblaka.

# Safeer Linux 1.0.24

One page can no longer take the computer down with it:

- Every tab has its own memory limit — half of the computer's RAM (6 GB on a 12 GB machine), so no normal page ever reaches it. At half of it WebKit releases caches, at three quarters everything that can be recreated, and only a runaway page that reaches the limit is stopped with a notice that says why, instead of the whole desktop swapping.
- The browser keeps an eye on every tab's process (CPU share and memory, read from the system every two seconds). A tab that stays over budget (70 % of a core or about 1 GB) for 15 seconds gets a small 🔥 in the tab strip with the numbers and a menu: put to sleep, reload or close. A heavy background tab that plays no sound is put to sleep after a minute (💤); selecting it loads the page again. Tabs playing music or video are never touched. Setting: `sleep_heavy_background_tabs`.
- `safeer://procesi` — the browser's own task manager: all tabs with CPU, memory, threads, state and actions, refreshing every three seconds.
- The browser's periodic page scripts (anti-adblock walls, cosmetic cleanup, invisible-overlay shield) no longer poll the whole page on fixed timers: a scan waits for an idle moment, does nothing while the tab is hidden, and is scheduled from how long the last one took. The overlay shield now asks the browser for the few elements on top instead of walking every element — the same cost on a ten-node page and on a social feed with a hundred thousand nodes. The anti-adblock script is not injected on Facebook, Messenger and Instagram at all.
- On desktops with systemd the browser runs in its own user cgroup scope (60 % of memory, slightly lower CPU weight): the kernel reclaims from Safeer first, so the rest of the desktop stays responsive even in the worst case. No privileges needed; skipped where there is no user manager.
- A rotating log at `~/.config/safeer-mint/safeer.log` (1 MB, three backups) keeps the browser's own diagnostics — which used to end in /dev/null under the desktop launcher.

Everything is local; the log contains the browser's messages, not a browsing history.

Slovensko: Ena stran ne more več zadušiti računalnika. Vsak zavihek ima svojo mejo pomnilnika – polovico RAM-a računalnika (6 GB pri 12 GB), tako da je nobena običajna stran ne doseže; pri polovici WebKit sprosti predpomnilnike, pri meji pa ustavi le pobeglo stran z obvestilom, namesto da bi celo namizje šlo v swap. Brskalnik vsaki dve sekundi meri procesor in pomnilnik vsakega zavihka: zavihek, ki je 15 s nad proračunom, dobi 🔥 z meniju (uspavaj, ponovno naloži, zapri); težek zavihek v ozadju brez zvoka po minuti zaspi (💤) in se ob kliku znova naloži, glasba in video pa se nikoli ne dotakneta. Nova stran `safeer://procesi` je upravitelj opravil brskalnika. Skripte za čiščenje oglasov in prekrivnih slojev ne pregledujejo več cele strani po urniku, ampak takrat, ko je brskalnik prost, in nikoli v skritih zavihkih; zaščita pred nevidnimi prekrivnimi sloji je zdaj enako hitra na ogromnih straneh. Brskalnik teče v lastni uporabniški cgroup, dnevnik pa je v `~/.config/safeer-mint/safeer.log`.

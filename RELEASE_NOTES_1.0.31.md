# Safeer Linux 1.0.31

**Safeer Link: name your devices the way you like, on each device separately.**

- Click a device in the Safeer Link window and choose Rename. The name is kept on this computer only (other family members can use their own names) and survives moving Safeer Link to another device. It is used in the device list, when sending a page and in notifications. An empty name restores the original.

Slovensko: V oknu Safeer Link klikni napravo in izberi Preimenuj. Ime velja samo na tem računalniku (drugi člani družine imajo lahko svoja imena) in ostane tudi, če se Safeer Link seli na drugo napravo. Pokaže se v seznamu, pri pošiljanju strani in v obvestilih. Prazno ime vrne izvirno.

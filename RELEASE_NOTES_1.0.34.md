# Safeer Linux 1.0.34

**Safeer Control 2.0.1: quiet in the background, and files for your TV.**

- Safeer Browser: no functional changes in this release (the shared Safeer Link page gained a section that only Safeer Control shows).
- Package **safeer-control** (2.0.1): Control now runs quietly in the background with an icon in the tray (Open / Start at login / Quit); after the first pairing it starts at login by itself, so your TV and phone see the computer whenever it is on. Closing the window only hides it.
- Safeer Control shares folders with your TV: pick folders in Control (or one click "Share Videos, Music and Pictures"); Safeer OS on the TV browses them and plays films, music and photos straight from this computer — over your home network only, encrypted, with a token the TV gets when it asks. Nothing is shared until you pick a folder.

Slovensko: Safeer Browser: v tej izdaji brez funkcionalnih sprememb (skupna stran Safeer Linka ima nov razdelek, ki ga kaže samo Safeer Control). Paket **safeer-control** (2.0.1): Control zdaj teče tiho v ozadju z ikono v pladnju (Odpri / Zaženi ob prijavi / Končaj); po prvi seznanitvi se zaganja ob prijavi sam, da televizor in telefon vidita računalnik, kadar je prižgan. Zapiranje okna ga le skrije. Safeer Control deli mape s televizorjem: izbereš mape v Controlu (ali z enim klikom »Deli Videi, Glasba in Slike«); Safeer OS na televizorju jih pregleduje in predvaja filme, glasbo in slike naravnost s tega računalnika – samo v domačem omrežju, šifrirano, z žetonom, ki ga televizor dobi ob zahtevi. Dokler ne izbereš mape, ni deljenega nič.
